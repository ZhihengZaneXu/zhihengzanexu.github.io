I served as the **Teaching Assistant** for this online course in Spring 2026, working with [Dr. Julie Ancis](https://people.njit.edu/profile/jra49). ## Course Overview

This upper-level online course examines the ethical implications of computers and information systems.

## Textbook

Michael J. Quinn, _Ethics for the Information Age_, 9th edition (2024).

- Attendance & participation — 15%
