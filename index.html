Zane Xu

PhD Candidate, Department of Informatics, NJIT

Hi, this is Zane Xu. I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Informatics at NJIT, working in the SPUR Lab under the supervision of Dr. Nathan Malkin. My research focuses on usable security and privacy. I am currently working on studying people’s privacy perspectives on always-recording technologies and the impact of Large Language Models (LLMs) on crowdsourcing research.
