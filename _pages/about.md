Hi, this is Zane Xu. 
I am a second-year PhD student in the Department of Informatics at [NJIT] (https://www.njit.edu/), working in the [SPUR Lab](https://www.spur.science/) under the supervision of [Dr. Nathan Malkin](https://malk.in). 
My research focuses on usable security and privacy. I am currently working on studying people's privacy perspectives on always-recording technologies and the impact of Large Language Models (LLMs) on crowdsourcing research.
